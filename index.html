Where is FITBULL GYM located?

FITBULL GYM is on the 3rd Floor of Newtown Plaza, Action Area 1B, New Town, Kolkata, West Bengal 700156.

Is FITBULL GYM a unisex gym?

Yes. FITBULL GYM is listed as a unisex gym in New Town, Kolkata.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Saturday: 6:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Sunday: 8:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Please contact the gym to confirm holiday or special-day timings.

What facilities are available?

FITBULL GYM has a 7,000+ sq. ft. workout area, modern equipment, trainer support, a dedicated stretching area, steam bath facility, shower rooms, lockers and parking.

What should I compare when choosing a gym in New Town?

Compare workout space, equipment, trainer support, available training options, amenities, location, opening hours and current membership terms. You can visit FITBULL GYM at Newtown Plaza to see the facility and discuss membership options.

Does FITBULL GYM offer ladies’ fitness facilities in New Town?

Yes. FITBULL GYM is a unisex gym with dedicated workout facilities and fitness programmes for women. It is not a women-only gym. Contact the team to learn about the available facilities and programmes.

What types of training are available?

Training options include personal training, strength and weight training, cardio, functional fitness, general fitness, bodybuilding and Zumba classes.
