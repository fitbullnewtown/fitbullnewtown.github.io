Does FITBULL GYM offer women's fitness facilities in New Town?

Yes. FITBULL GYM provides dedicated workout facilities and fitness programmes for women within its unisex fitness centre at Newtown Plaza, Action Area 1B, New Town, Kolkata.

Is FITBULL GYM a women-only gym?

No. FITBULL GYM is a unisex gym. It also provides dedicated workout facilities and fitness programmes for women.

Where is FITBULL GYM located?

FITBULL GYM is on the 3rd Floor of Newtown Plaza, Action Area 1B, New Town, Kolkata, West Bengal 700156.

What training options are available?

Training options include strength and weight training, cardio, functional fitness, personal training, general fitness and bodybuilding. Contact the team for details about current programmes.

Monday to Saturday: 6:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Sunday: 8:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Please contact the gym to confirm holiday or special-day timings.
