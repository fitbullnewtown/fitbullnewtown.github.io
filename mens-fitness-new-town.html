A well-equipped training environment for strength, muscle development and everyday fitness.

FITBULL GYM is a unisex gym and fitness centre on the 3rd Floor of Newtown Plaza, Action Area 1B. Men can train across strength and weight training, bodybuilding, cardio and functional fitness, with trainer support available for different fitness levels. The facility includes a dedicated stretching area and member amenities such as a steam bath, shower rooms and lockers.

This is not a men-only gym. It is a shared fitness facility for men and women, with training options that support different routines and fitness goals.

Is FITBULL GYM a men-only gym?

No. FITBULL GYM is a unisex gym and fitness centre. Men and women can use the gym, and dedicated workout facilities and programmes are also available for women.

Can men do weight training and bodybuilding at FITBULL?

Yes. Weight training, strength training and bodybuilding are among the training options listed by FITBULL GYM.

Is trainer support available?

FITBULL GYM provides trainer support. Contact the team to confirm the current personal-training arrangements and membership inclusions.

Where is FITBULL GYM located?

FITBULL GYM is on the 3rd Floor of Newtown Plaza, Action Area 1B, New Town, Kolkata, West Bengal 700156.
